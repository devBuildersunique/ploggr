# Bundled visual assets

All four PNGs were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project,
then copied into the repository. They are illustrations/sample photography, not
reference screenshots. The app loads its artwork and font from the bundle.

- `assets/images/illustrations/splash.png`: splash park and plogger silhouettes.
- `assets/images/illustrations/plogging.png`: first onboarding illustration.
- `assets/images/illustrations/together.png`: third onboarding illustration.
- `assets/images/demo/litter.png`: explicitly labeled sample camera/report/post photo.
- `assets/fonts/NunitoSans.ttf`: locally bundled Google Fonts Nunito Sans variable
  font, licensed under the accompanying `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
- `lib/shared/widgets/eco_art.dart`: Flutter-drawn wordmark, foliage, landscape,
  campus, trophy, badges, and fictional avatars.
- `lib/shared/widgets/preview_map.dart`: locally drawn, non-geographic street map.

## Generation prompts

### Plogging
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: locally bundled onboarding illustration
for the Ploggr mobile environmental fitness app. Create one polished flat
vector-style editorial illustration, landscape 3:2 composition: a young South
Asian man with dark hair, green t-shirt, dark forest green trousers, green
backpack and white sneakers bending forward to pick up a small discarded bottle
beside a dark green rubbish sack in a leafy city park. Stylized organic shapes,
clean edges, gentle green gradients, friendly sophisticated mobile app art.
Layered pale sage and lime hills, bushes, rounded trees, subtle distant city
silhouettes, airy off-white (#F8FBF6) sky. Rich leaf green and forest green palette,
warm tan skin. Subject fills the central 65 percent with generous pale sky above.
No text, no UI, no borders, no device frame, no watermark. Match the environmental
vector illustration style of a professionally designed eco fitness onboarding screen.

### Together
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: landscape 3:2 onboarding art for an
environmental community mobile app. One elegant flat vector-style illustration
of four human hands entering diagonally from the bottom and sides, cupped
together supporting a little dark soil globe with a large fresh green two-leaf
sapling growing from it. Two green sleeves and two pale cream sleeves, diverse
warm tan skin colors. The plant is centered and fills the upper center; complete
hands and forearms visible. Layered pale sage and light lime rolling hills,
small rounded tree silhouettes and soft clouds behind, off-white #F8FBF6 sky.
Fresh leaf green and deep forest green accents, clean vector edges, subtle organic
gradients. Friendly professional aesthetic, generous breathing space. No text,
no UI, no frame, no watermark.

### Sample litter photo
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: sample litter photograph for a
clearly marked demo camera in an environmental cleanup mobile app. Portrait 2:3
photograph, close low camera view of one discarded slightly crushed clear plastic
water bottle with a pale blue blank label and bright green cap, lying diagonally
on dry earthy gravel among small twigs and fallen leaves on a woodland walking
path. Bottle occupies the middle half of the image with its cap toward lower
left. Soft blurry lush green vegetation behind, natural dappled daylight.
Beautiful realistic documentary photography, shallow depth of field, detailed
gravel and transparent plastic. No people, no text, no branding, no watermark,
no UI overlays, no borders.

### Splash footer
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: footer illustration for a mobile
environmental fitness splash screen. Landscape 3:2 vector-style illustration:
two small dark forest-green silhouettes of adult ploggers walking toward the
right on a gently rolling park path, each holding a green litter collection bag.
One woman with ponytail and one man, friendly natural walking poses, full bodies,
figures in the center lower half. Beautiful layered fresh lime and sage rolling
hills, rounded trees on both sides, extremely pale green distant city buildings,
simple soft organic vector shapes, off-white #F8FBF6 empty sky across the upper
third. Strong flat graphic silhouettes rather than detailed faces. Match polished
professional eco-app botanical illustration. Palette deep forest #103F20, rich
leaf green #218600, lime #93CC62 and pale sage. Clean composition, no text, no logo,
no frame, no UI, no watermark.
